    Since the hole in the wall

    A lifelong dream, cooked daily

    Rice & Things began as a takeaway people knew simply as the hole in the wall. It grew into a restaurant and bar without losing the thing that made it worth queueing for: a Jamaican chef who refuses to cut the seasoning.

    Chef Neufville also bottles a range of Jamaican wines from family recipes — the sorrel among them — sold only here in Bristol. His foundation has sent over $5 million worth of medical equipment to May Pen Hospital in Jamaica.
